DAILY PROGRESS REPORT
Indravati River Pumping Station Project
Date: 05 September 2026
Report No: DPR-2026-248
Submitted by: Site Engineer, Civil Division

=====================================================
SECTION 1 — PUMP HOUSE CIVIL WORKS
=====================================================

1.1 Foundation Work:
Pump house foundation work is progressing very slowly due to continued material delivery issues. 
Reinforcement steel bars (Fe-500) for the grade beam casting have been only partially delivered.
Out of required 28 MT, only 18 MT has arrived at site. Supplier has promised balance delivery by 
September 12, 2026, but this has already been delayed twice. Current foundation progress stands at 
approximately 72% against the planned 100% as on date. Concrete casting for the grade beam could 
not be completed and only 55% of planned casting is done.

1.2 Column Construction (Level 1):
Column concreting on Axis-A has been completed (100%). However, column concreting on Axis-B is only 
60% complete against the planned 100%. Delay is attributed to formwork material shortage — the 
contractor's formwork has not been returned from the other ongoing site at Sector-IV. Estimated 
completion by Sept 10, 2026 if formwork is returned in time.

1.3 Roof Slab Works:
Roof slab shuttering has not commenced as planned. No progress as the structural drawing revision 
(Rev-3) from Design is pending approval from the PMC. Only 38% of planned progress achieved.
Equipment (concrete batching plant) is also under maintenance and will be available from September 8.

=====================================================
SECTION 2 — RISING MAIN PIPELINE
=====================================================

2.1 Trench Excavation:
Pipeline trench excavation from Km 0 to Km 2.0 is substantially complete. Work at Km 2+000 to 
Km 4+000 is ongoing. Overall trench excavation is at 90% of plan.

2.2 Pipe Laying — 600mm MS Pipes:
Pipe laying has been severely affected by delay in pipe delivery from the manufacturer 
(M/s Jindal Pipes, Raipur). Out of 480 pipes required for Phase-1, only 112 pipes have been 
delivered to site as of today. The manufacturing delay is attributed to a machine breakdown at 
the factory. Pipe laying is currently at 22% against the planned 60%. This is a critical delay. 
First 200 metres of pipe laying completed from Km 0+000 to Km 0+200. Further laying is stalled.

2.3 Thrust Block Construction:
Thrust block work has not been started as pipe laying is incomplete. Planned progress was 35% 
but actual is only 10%.

=====================================================
SECTION 3 — PUMP INSTALLATION
=====================================================

3.1 Pump Foundation:
Pump foundation concrete casting is progressing but behind schedule. Civil work is at 55% 
against planned 80%. Reason: RCC design for pump pedestal was revised and contractor received 
the revised drawing only on September 3, 2026. This caused a delay of 12 days.

3.2 Pump Delivery:
Main centrifugal pumps (4 nos.) have been supplied by M/s Kirloskar Brothers Limited. 
2 out of 4 pumps have been delivered to site. Remaining 2 pumps are expected by September 15, 2026.
Overall pump supply delivery at 48% against planned 70%.

3.3 Pump Installation:
Pump installation and alignment work is at 5% as pump foundation is still incomplete. 
Planned progress was 30%.

=====================================================
SECTION 4 — ELECTRICAL WORKS
=====================================================

4.1 HT Line Erection:
HT line erection is on track. 50% of erection complete against planned 60%. Minor variation 
is expected to be recovered.

4.2 Transformer Installation:
Transformer has been delivered to site and civil foundation is ready. Installation work will 
commence on September 8, 2026. Current progress 20% against planned 25% — on track.

4.3 LT Panel & MCC:
Not started as transformer installation is pending. Planned progress 10%, actual 5%.

=====================================================
SECTION 5 — INTAKE STRUCTURE
=====================================================

5.1 Screen Chamber Walls:
Screen chamber wall construction is progressing well. Currently at 70% against planned 85%.
Slight shortfall due to heavy rainfall during August 28-31 (78mm recorded).

5.2 Stop Log Gate Delivery:
Stop log gate has not been delivered. Order was placed with M/s Triveni Engineering in July 2026 
but delivery is stuck due to transportation clearance issues. Supply at 25% of planned 60%.
This is likely to delay screen chamber completion.

=====================================================

- Pump installation to commence once foundation complete (expected Sept 18)
